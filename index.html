Q1Q: How much does a house extension cost in Worcester? A: The cost of a house extension in Worcester typically ranges from £1,500 to £2,200 per square metre for a single storey build, depending on specification, ground conditions, and finishes chosen. A double storey extension costs more overall but typically works out at a lower cost per square metre than two separate single storey builds. As a general guide, a well-specified 25m² single storey kitchen extension in Worcester might cost in the region of £45,000–£55,000 fully completed including all building regulation fees, structural work and making good internally. We provide detailed fixed price quotations at no charge — call 01905 831778 to arrange a site visit.
Q2Q: Do I need planning permission for an extension in Worcester? A: Many single storey extensions in Worcester can be built under Permitted Development rights without a full planning application. For a detached house, a single storey rear extension up to 4 metres deep generally qualifies. For semi-detached and terraced properties, the limit is 3 metres. However, if your property is in a conservation area — and Worcester city centre has several — Permitted Development rights may be more restricted. Side extensions and double storey extensions nearly always require full planning permission. We advise on planning requirements as part of our free initial consultation, and have extensive experience working with Worcester City Council's planning department.
Q3Q: How long does it take to build an extension in Worcester? A: For a typical single storey rear extension in Worcester, from breaking ground to practical completion is usually between 10 and 14 weeks, depending on the size and complexity of the build. Double storey extensions generally take between 16 and 24 weeks. Before we start any project, we provide a written programme showing the key stages and expected dates so you know exactly what to expect and when.
Q4Q: What areas around Worcester do you cover for extension work? A: We cover the full Worcester area including the city centre, Barbourne, St Johns, Bransford Road, Henwick, Claines, Fernhill Heath, Droitwich, Pershore, Upton upon Severn, and all surrounding villages within Worcestershire. For extension projects we generally cover a radius of approximately 25 miles from our Callow End base. If you are unsure whether we cover your area, call us on 01905 831778 and we will confirm.

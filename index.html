2
FAQ bank — copy onto service pages
Pick 3–5 per page. They capture "People also ask" search results.
▼
  • Q: How much does a house extension cost in Worcester?
    Suggested answer
    A single storey extension in Worcester typically costs between £1,500–£2,200 per square metre depending on specification, finishes and complexity. A two-storey extension costs more per square metre but gives significantly more space for the investment. We provide fixed price quotations — contact us for a free, no-obligation estimate.
  • Q: Do I need planning permission for an extension in Worcestershire?
    Suggested answer
    Many extensions in Worcestershire can be built under Permitted Development rights, meaning no full planning application is required. Single storey rear extensions up to 4m (detached) or 3m (semi/terraced) often qualify. We advise on planning requirements as part of our free initial consultation.
  • Q: How long does a house extension take to build?
    Suggested answer
    A single storey extension typically takes 10–14 weeks from groundwork to completion. A two-storey extension is usually 16–22 weeks. We provide a clear project programme before starting so you know exactly what to expect.
  • Q: Do you cover Malvern and the surrounding villages?
    Suggested answer
    Yes — we regularly work across Great Malvern, Malvern Wells, West Malvern, Colwall, Upton upon Severn, Pershore, Droitwich and across the Malvern Hills area, as well as throughout Worcester and wider Worcestershire.

Content strategy — what to post and when
5 content types, posted 2–3 times per week. Consistency beats frequency.
▼
  • Post type 1: Project progress (weekly, Monday or Tuesday)
    A photo from any active site — foundations, frame stage, roof on, completion. One photo, short caption. Most-shared post type in local building groups.
    Example caption template
    Good progress on this rear single storey extension in [TOWN] this week — steelwork going in for the roof today. Lovely project opening up the kitchen into the garden. Thinking about an extension? Call 01905 831778. #extensions #worcester #malvern
  • Post type 2: Before & after (bi-weekly)
    Side-by-side photo of before and after a project. Use Canva (free) to create a split image. These get shared constantly by homeowners. Even older projects work — dig out any before photos.
    FreeHighest engagement type
  • Post type 3: Educational posts (monthly)
    Example educational post
    Did you know — in many cases you can build a single storey rear extension without full planning permission under Permitted Development rights? For a detached house, up to 4 metres deep. For a semi-detached or terraced, up to 3 metres. We can advise as part of a free initial chat. Call 01905 831778.
  • Post type 4: Credibility posts (monthly)
    Example credibility post
    Court Property has been building homes and extensions across Worcestershire since 1997. Over 250 homes. Over 50 contract projects. The same core team, the same trusted subcontractors. When you hire us for your extension, you're getting 25 years of local building experience. 📞 01905 831778
  • Post type 5: Local area posts (monthly)
    Reference local landmarks and villages: "Working in the Malvern Hills today — hard to beat the office views." Tag the location in every post. These signal locality to the Facebook algorithm and resonate with local audiences.
    Free
